The links you saved for later are scattered across notes, chats, and open tabs. Paste one into ClipNote and it reads the title and image, turns it into a card, and keeps it in one place.

■ Paste and done
Drop in a URL and the title, description, and image are pulled in for you. Don't like the title? Change it right there.

■ Tags keep it findable
Tag each clip and filter your list by tag. Select several at once to add or remove tags together.

■ Share in one line
Sharing copies the title and the link — nothing else to clean up before you paste.

■ Sign in later, or not at all
Clips are saved on this device without an account. Sign in later to move them over, and declining to move them never deletes anything.

■ Save from anywhere
Pick ClipNote in the share sheet from Safari or any other app, and the link is already in the field.

■ Four languages
Korean, English, Japanese, and Simplified Chinese. Switch in Settings — no restart needed.
