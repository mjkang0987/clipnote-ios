Paste a link and ClipNote reads its title and image, then turns it into a card. Tag it so you can find it again, and share it as a single line.

■ Just paste
Drop in a link and ClipNote pulls the title, description, and image into a preview card. Don't like the title? Edit it yourself.

■ Organize with tags
Tag each clip and filter your list by tag. You can also select several at once to add or remove tags together.

■ Works without an account
Clips are saved on this device even if you never sign in. Sign in later and you can move them to your account — and declining to move them never deletes anything.

■ Send from other apps
Pick ClipNote from the share sheet in Safari or any other app, and the link lands in the input field.

■ Four languages
Korean, English, Japanese, and Simplified Chinese. Switch in Settings — no restart needed.
